## who am i

 - i think around the given task or problem and try to find the best fit solution. 
 - i love being productive, keep my work organized and clean. 
 - i would like my work organized in such a way that it follows a pattern and its easy to recollect and navigate. 
 - i try to identify and use repeatable patterns to make my work more efficient and effective. 
 - i will be using this persona when not working as a software engineer (which is my profession)

## my working style
 - i work in a structured manner, breaking down complex problems into smaller, manageable tasks.

 - i believe in the concept of "getting things done" and try to complete tasks efficiently.
 - when working with AI i would like to identify repeatable patterns and create "Skills" where appropriate to automate repetitive tasks.
 - As an AI Assistant, i would like you to Ask-me-Questions on the tasks or problems which i am working on with you so that together we can develop a better understanding and solution.

## my output formatting style
 - i will be working on multiple or a variety of task and problems at personal level.
 - i will be organizing templates as i evolve with my work with AI

### when working on Excel or Spreadsheet as output
 - always include a summary sheet as the first sheet
 - also include a sheet for references, any helper sheets as appropriate
 - the tabular data should always include header row
 - the data should be formatted as alternate rows with different colors for better readability
 - use elegant coloring style, especially use pastel colors for better visual appeal

### when working on Powerpoint or presentation as output
 - use elegant and professional templates
 - use landscape orientation
 - a main content slide should include a thin title bar at the top and a thin footer at the bottom
   - the main area should occupy most about 70% of the slide and be used for elegant visuals
   - the remaining 30% or less should be used on the right of that slide for a numbered narrative to explain the central visual
   - the narrative should be concise and to the point; use as less words as possible to explain
   - the central visual should be drawn with boxes and lines, not with images or graphics
 - use consistent fonts, pastel color schemes
 - avoid using high-quality images and graphics
 - avoid using animations and transitions

### when working on a Single-Page-HTML as output
 - use consistent fonts, pastel color schemes
